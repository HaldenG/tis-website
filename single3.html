PRESENTATION OF AUTICONT

January 14th 2026

A stressful but important process of designing and proposing a system. We had to take into account every single perspective of the users experience. Our system is about helping the disadvantaged community. At first we decided on both the ADHD community and the people with autism. Then we narrowed down the scope of the system to just focusing on autism for now. It took weeks upon weeks to design the system, and redoing the whole thing again.

We ended up from a website to a mobile app that utilizes the use of a smartwatch's heartbeat sensor to read the heartbeat of an autism indivdual, to take the measure the heartrate and notifies the guardian that their child is either having a tantrum or doing sports. This was initially our problem. Then we decided to change it from the user's end, to make sure that the guardian was aware that their children is going to perform physical activities that will result in spikes of the hearts BPM.
